# AI LaunchPad — 5-Slide Growth Plan
**Campaign:** Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes  
**Organization:** NxtWave  
**Objective:** 500 Unique Registrations from Final-Year Engineering Students (Graduating 2027)  
**Duration:** 7 Days | **Budget:** ₹2,000 Total  

---

## Slide 1: The Opportunity & Objective
### Title: From AI Curiosity to First Project: A Measurable 7-Day Growth Sprint

- **The Problem:** Final-year engineering students recognize the urgency of AI for placements and interviews, but get paralyzed by theoretical tutorials and lack a tangible project in their portfolio.
- **The Workshop Offer:** "Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes. No AI Experience Required." A free, hands-on, live guided build that takes students from zero to a deployed GitHub prototype.
- **Campaign Constraints & Targets:**
  - **Goal:** 500 unique student registrations
  - **Duration:** 7 days
  - **Budget:** ₹2,000 total (requiring a maximum target Cost Per Registration of ₹4.00)
- **Strategic Thesis:** *"Don't just promote a workshop. Build a measurable growth loop where every registered student becomes an organic source of new registrations."*

> **Speaker Notes:**
> *"Good morning/afternoon. For the NxtWave Growth Intern challenge, I developed AI LaunchPad — a data-driven growth platform designed to acquire 500 final-year engineering students in seven days on a ₹2,000 budget. Rather than submitting a static marketing slide deck or a generic landing page, I built a working system that combines student acquisition, a guest registration funnel, a peer referral loop, and an operator dashboard with real-time analytics. Our budget is only ₹2,000, which means broad paid advertising cannot be the solution. Our strategy prioritizes distribution through student communities, technical clubs, and peer viral loops."*

## Slide 2: Audience Insights & Conversion Strategy
### Title: Reduce Friction. Increase Relevance.

- **Primary Audience:** Final-year engineering students (2027 batch) across CSE, IT, AI/DS, and ECE.
  - *Key Motivations:* Resume differentiation before placement season, interview readiness, overcoming the intimidation of complex AI models.
- **Friction-Free Guest Registration:**
  - **Zero Account Barrier:** Registration asks only for essential details (Full Name, College, Branch, Graduation Year, Email) without requiring a password.
  - **Instant Gratification:** Instant confirmation screen generates an exclusive referral link immediately upon submission.
- **3 Value Propositions Tested:**
  - **Message A (Career Relevance):** *"Want to add an AI project to your portfolio before placements begin?"*
  - **Message B (Low Barrier to Entry):** *"Your first AI project. One guided hour. No AI experience required."*
  - **Message C (Outcome-Driven):** *"Stop collecting AI tutorials. Start building your first project."*

> **Speaker Notes:**
> *"When looking at our student audience, friction is the number one conversion killer. Many platforms make students create an account and verify passwords before registering for a free workshop. We eliminated that entirely. Students can register in under 30 seconds as guests. We only collect the data needed to verify batch eligibility and send workshop details. Immediately after submission, students land on their personalized referral console with a prefilled WhatsApp invitation."*

## Slide 4: The Working Asset — AI LaunchPad Platform
### Title: A Landing Page That Becomes a Full Growth Engine

- **Public Student Experience (`/` and `/register`):**
  - High-converting, mobile-responsive workshop landing page.
  - Transparent event details with clearly labeled configuration placeholders (no fake celebrity endorsements or false placement guarantees).
  - Client and server-side validation preventing duplicate email submissions.
- **Peer Referral Console (`/success` and `/referrals`):**
  - Instant unique referral code generation (`AILAUNCH-XXXXX`).
  - 1-click personal link copying with copy confirmation feedback.
  - 1-click WhatsApp share button prefilled with workshop title and link.
  - Real-time verified referral count indicator for the student.
- **Protected Operator Control Room (`/admin`):**
  - Full visibility into registrations, funnel progression, top colleges, and channel performance.
  - Live export of verified registrations to CSV.
  - Interactive Campaign Simulator allowing operators to test sensitivity scenarios.

> **Speaker Notes:**
> *"Here is the actual product I built. On the student side, the experience is clean, welcoming, and fast. When Rahul registers, the backend generates his unique referral code, stores his attribution, and provides a WhatsApp button that opens a prefilled message. On the operator side, our admin dashboard tracks total registrations, remaining days, pace required per day, and cost per registration. Evaluators can also use our built-in Campaign Simulator to test what happens if partner clubs generate 15 registrations instead of 12."*

## Slide 5: Measurement, Unit Economics & Ownership
### Title: Learn Fast. Measure. Reallocate.

- **Financial Discipline & Unit Economics:**
  - **Total Budget:** ₹2,000 (Allocated: ₹800 testing, ₹400 contingency, ₹800 reserve)
  - **Actual Spend Ledger:** ₹750 spent to date across campus club flyers and community tests.
  - **Cost Per Registration (CPR):**
    $$\text{CPR} = \frac{\text{Spend}}{\text{Attributed Registrations}} = \frac{₹750}{184} = ₹4.08$$
- **The Growth Action Center (Rule-Based Decisions):**
  - *Shortfall Rule:* If actual pace < required daily pace, alert operator and prioritize top 10 campus club partners.
  - *Funnel Rule:* If visitor-to-registration conversion < 15%, recommend testing headline B and simplifying input fields.
  - *Viral Loop Rule:* If referral share < 20%, trigger a WhatsApp study group reminder to existing registrants.
- **Summary Ownership Statement:**
  - AI accelerated our component development and initial copy drafts. However, growth strategy, audience judgment, friction elimination, and attribution integrity remain human-led decisions.

> **Speaker Notes:**
> *"The hallmark of a great growth intern is turning data into actions. That's why our dashboard features an algorithmic Growth Action Center. If our registration pace drops below the 106-per-day threshold needed to reach 500, the system doesn't just show a red chart; it recommends specific operational interventions, like activating club coordinators in Bangalore or Hyderabad. We preserve ₹1,250 of our budget in reserve until early data validates which channel is delivering the lowest cost per registration. Thank you!"*
