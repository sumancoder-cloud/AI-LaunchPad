# AI LaunchPad — 3-Minute Video Demonstration Script
**Project:** AI LaunchPad — The 500-Student Growth Engine  
**Role:** NxtWave Growth Intern Submission  
**Target Duration:** 2 minutes 50 seconds – 3 minutes (approx. 380 words)  

---

### Timing & Screen Flow:

```
[0:00 - 0:25] Introduction & The Core Challenge
[0:25 - 0:55] Growth Strategy & The 500-Student Model
[0:55 - 1:35] Student Experience: Landing, Guest Registration & Referral Engine
[1:35 - 2:10] Operator Control Room: KPIs, Funnel & Growth Action Center
[2:10 - 2:40] Campaign Simulator & Critical AI Decisions
[2:40 - 3:00] Closing Reflections & Next Steps
```

---

### [0:00 – 0:25] The Problem & The Core Idea
**Screen View:** Candidate on camera (or split-screen with the landing page hero).  
**Script:**  
> *"Hi, I'm excited to present my submission for the NxtWave Growth Intern challenge!*  
> *The assignment was to acquire 500 final-year engineering students for a free, 60-minute practical AI workshop within seven days on a ₹2,000 budget.*  
> *Instead of submitting just a five-slide presentation or a generic landing page, I built **AI LaunchPad** — a full-stack growth platform that connects student acquisition, guest registration, a viral referral loop, and real-time operator analytics into one cohesive engine."*

---

### [0:25 – 0:55] The Growth Strategy
**Screen View:** Show Slide 3 (The 5-channel mathematical breakdown) or the Campaign Simulator view.  
**Script:**  
> *"Because our budget is only ₹2,000, our target Cost Per Registration is ₹4. Broad paid advertising was out of the question.*  
> *My acquisition model breaks down the 500-student goal across five organic, high-trust channels:*  
> *240 registrations through 20 engineering club partnerships;*  
> *100 through active student WhatsApp groups;*  
> *100 through our peer referral loop;*  
> *and 60 through LinkedIn and direct outreach.*  
> *Every channel uses distinct UTM parameters so attribution is deterministic and never double-counted."*

---

### [0:55 – 1:35] The Student Experience & Viral Referral Loop
**Screen View:** Live demo in browser. Start at `http://localhost:3000/`, click "Register", fill the form with a test student name, submit, and show the success confirmation screen.  
**Script:**  
> *"Let's look at the student experience.*  
> *On the landing page, the value proposition is crystal clear: 'Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes. No AI Experience Required.'*  
> *To eliminate drop-off, I chose a guest registration model. Students don't need to create a password or verify an account to save their seat. They simply enter their details, college, branch, and graduation year.*  
> *When Rahul registers, the system checks for duplicate emails, creates his record, and instantly generates his personal referral code — like `AILAUNCH-K7P4Q`.*  
> *He can copy his link with one click, or tap the WhatsApp button to share a prefilled invitation directly with his engineering study groups. As his friends register, his referral counter updates in real time."*

---

### [1:35 – 2:10] Operator Control Room & Growth Action Center
**Screen View:** Navigate to `/admin`. Show the Overview KPIs, the Daily Registration bar chart, the Measured Funnel, and zoom in on the Growth Action Center cards.  
**Script:**  
> *"Now let's switch to the operator side at `/admin`.*  
> *Here, the campaign lead has complete visibility: total registrations against the 500 target, daily acquisition momentum, conversion rates, and the spend ledger.*  
> *What makes this dashboard unique is the **Growth Action Center**. Rather than just displaying passive charts, it translates live data into actionable decisions.*  
> *For example, if our daily pace falls below the 106 registrations needed per day, the system alerts the operator and recommends prioritizing outreach to technical clubs in top engineering hubs. If the visitor-to-registration rate drops below 15%, it suggests A/B testing our secondary headline."*

---

### [2:10 – 2:40] Campaign Simulator & Critical AI Reflections
**Screen View:** Navigate to `/admin/strategy` and show the Campaign Simulator adjusting inputs.  
**Script:**  
> *"We also built a built-in **Campaign Simulator**. An operator can test alternative scenarios — for example, modeling what happens if campus clubs generate 15 registrations instead of 12.*  
> *Throughout this project, I used AI as an accelerator, but exercised deliberate judgment.*  
> *When AI suggested requiring user accounts and passwords, I deliberately rejected it because reducing registration friction was critical for our conversion rate.*  
> *And when AI suggested spending our ₹2,000 budget on social ads immediately, I held ₹1,250 in reserve until our organic channel performance was validated."*

---

### [2:40 – 3:00] Closing & Summary
**Screen View:** Candidate on camera with the `/admin` dashboard in background.  
**Script:**  
> *"If I had another 24 hours, I would conduct a live headline test with student cohorts and set up automated WhatsApp webhook notifications for top referrers.*  
> *To me, growth isn't just about getting clicks — it's about building a scalable system, measuring unit economics, and making rapid, data-backed decisions.*  
> *Thank you, and I look forward to contributing to NxtWave's growth team!"*

---

### Tips for Recording:
1. **Audio:** Use headphones with a mic in a quiet room. Speak clearly and with genuine enthusiasm.
2. **Resolution:** Record in 1080p full screen (1920x1080).
3. **Demo Readiness:** Start the demo with the landing page open, fill out a sample registration, copy the referral link, and open `/admin` to show the numbers live.
